How Unsub works

Nothing happens to your email unless you choose a sender and tap Zap.

Connect Gmail

Sign in with Google. No password to create. Unsub scans your recent emails in Gmail's Promotions and Updates tabs for messages that contain an unsubscribe link.

Choose who goes

See every mailing-list sender and how many emails they've sent you. Clear many at once with History Clean, or decide one by one with Daily Swipe.

Zap

Unsub sends the sender an unsubscribe request and moves all of their mailing-list emails to Trash, recoverable for 30 days. Most senders stop within 2 days.

Why Unsub needs Gmail access

Unsub asks Google for one permission (gmail.modify). Google describes it broadly, so here is exactly what Unsub does and doesn't do with it.

Unsub uses it to

  • Read the sender name, sender address and subject line of mailing-list emails, so you can see who's emailing you
  • Read the standard unsubscribe link senders include in their emails
  • Move emails from senders you choose to Trash

Unsub never

  • Reads the body or attachments of your emails
  • Sends email from your account
  • Permanently deletes anything
  • Sells your data, uses it for ads, or uses it to train AI

Unsub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read our full Privacy Policy. You can disconnect anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
