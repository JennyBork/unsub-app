🧼 Unsub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Unsub (“Unsub,” “we,” “us”) is operated by Jennifer Borkowski, based in Illinois, USA. Unsub helps you unsubscribe from mailing lists and clean promotional email out of your inbox. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. You can reach us at jenny@jentlestay.com.

1. What Unsub does

After you sign in with your email provider, Unsub lists the senders of promotional and mailing-list emails in your mailbox. You choose which senders to remove, and Unsub unsubscribes you from them where the sender supports it and moves their emails to your Trash folder. Nothing is removed unless you select it and press the Zap button.

2. Google user data we access

Unsub currently supports Gmail. When you connect your Gmail account, we request one Google permission, gmail.modify. Google’s approval screen describes this permission broadly, but Unsub accesses only:

  • Your email address, so we can show which account is connected and match it to your subscription.
  • Mailing-list email details – for emails in your Promotions and Updates categories that contain an unsubscribe link: the message ID, Gmail category, sender name, sender address and subject line.
  • Unsubscribe information – the standard List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers that senders include in those emails.
  • Google access credentials (OAuth tokens) that let the app act on your mailbox while you use it.

Unsub does not access the body or attachments of your emails, your contacts, or any email outside the mailing-list emails described above. The only aggregated data we keep is anonymous counts (for example, “14 emails moved to Trash”) in our server logs, with no email address or other personal information attached.

3. How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide the features you ask for:

  • Showing your mailing-list senders and how many emails each has sent you.
  • Unsubscribing you – when you zap a sender, we use their unsubscribe information to send a one-click unsubscribe request to the sender’s own unsubscribe link.
  • Moving emails to Trash – only emails from senders you choose. They stay recoverable in Gmail for 30 days. Unsub never permanently deletes email and never sends email from your account.
  • Checking your subscription – we look up your connected email address in our Stripe account.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, to build profiles, for credit or lending decisions, or to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not let anyone read your data except as described here, as needed for security, or as required by law.

4. How we share (transfer) Google user data

We do not sell Google user data, and we never transfer it to advertisers, data brokers, information resellers or AI/ML services. Data is shared only with these types of parties, only as needed to run Unsub:

  • Our payment processor (Stripe) receives your connected email address so your subscription can be created and checked. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen by Unsub. See Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider (Render) runs our server, which processes your data in memory on our behalf.
  • The sender you unsubscribe from – when you zap a sender that supports one-click unsubscribe, our server contacts the unsubscribe link that sender included in their own email. We add no information about you beyond what the sender already put in that link.
  • Legal requirements – we may disclose information if required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Unsub’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How we protect Google user data

  • All connections to Unsub use HTTPS encryption.
  • Google access credentials are kept only in our server’s memory. They are never written to a database or disk, and each person’s credentials are kept separate so one person’s login can never be used for another.
  • Our secret keys are stored in protected environment settings, not in our code.
  • Our server logs do not record your email address, sender details or any email content.
  • Unsubscribe requests are only sent to secure (https) public web addresses, never to private or internal ones.
  • Access to our systems is limited to the app’s operator.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Email details (senders, subjects, unsubscribe information) are read while the app is working and shown to you. We do not save them on our servers.
  • Google access credentials are held in server memory only for your session and deleted when it ends (after at most 7 days), when you press Reset, or when our server restarts.
  • In your browser, a session identifier, your email address and the list of senders you chose to keep are saved in local storage so the app remembers you. Pressing Reset removes your session and email address; clearing your browser data removes everything.
  • Server logs contain only anonymous counts and no Google user data.
  • Billing records (your email address and subscription history) are kept by Stripe for as long as needed for billing, tax and legal purposes.

To delete your data: press Reset in the app and revoke Unsub’s access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops Unsub from reaching your mailbox. To delete your billing record, email jenny@jentlestay.com and we will delete what we hold about you within 30 days, other than records we must keep by law.

7. Your emails stay recoverable

Emails Unsub moves to Trash can be restored from your Gmail Trash folder within 30 days.

8. Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe’s secure pages and are never seen or stored by Unsub. Your subscription is linked to your connected email address.

9. Children

Unsub is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Unsub changes, for example when we add support for more email providers. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and we will ask for your approval again where new permissions are needed.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: jenny@jentlestay.com
