🧼 Unsub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Unsub (“Unsub,” “we,” “us”) is operated by Jennifer Borkowski, based in Illinois, USA. Unsub helps you clean promotional email out of your inbox. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. You can reach us at jenny@jentlestay.com.

1. What Unsub does

After you sign in with your email provider, Unsub lists the senders of promotional and mailing-list emails in your mailbox. You choose which senders to remove, and Unsub unsubscribes you from them where the sender supports it and moves their emails to your Trash folder. Nothing is removed unless you select it and press the Zap button.

2. Information we access

Currently Unsub supports Gmail. When you connect your Gmail account and approve access, we request one Google permission, gmail.modify. Google’s approval screen describes this permission broadly, but Unsub uses it only to:

  • Find mailing-list emails – emails in your Promotions and Updates categories that contain an unsubscribe link – and read their sender name, sender address and subject line so we can show them to you.
  • Find unsubscribe links – we read the standard unsubscribe information senders include in their emails’ technical headers.
  • Move emails to Trash – only emails from senders you choose. Emails in Trash stay recoverable in Gmail for 30 days.

When you zap a sender that supports one-click unsubscribe, our server contacts that sender’s own unsubscribe link to tell them to stop emailing you. This does not send an email from your account, and we add no information about you beyond what the sender put in that link.

Unsub does not read the body or attachments of your emails, and does not send email on your behalf.

We also receive your email address from Google so we can show which account is connected.

3. Information we store

  • Access tokens. To act on your mailbox during your session, our server keeps the access credentials Google gives us in the server’s memory. They are not saved to a database and are discarded when the session ends (after at most 7 days) or when the server restarts.
  • In your browser. A session identifier and your email address are saved in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in. Pressing the Reset button removes them.
  • Server logs. Our hosting logs may record operational events such as the email address that connected, and the number of senders and emails processed. We do not log the contents of your emails.

4. How we use information

We use the information only to provide and improve Unsub’s features that you request: showing you promotional senders, moving the emails you select to Trash, and keeping you signed in. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes. We do not use email content to build profiles or train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Unsub’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe’s secure pages and are never seen or stored by Unsub. To check whether your subscription is active, Unsub looks up your connected email address in our Stripe account, and your subscription is linked to that address. Stripe’s handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe’s privacy policy.

6. Service providers

Unsub is hosted on Render, which processes requests on our behalf. Stripe processes payments. These providers may handle data only as needed to run their services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect any time. Press Reset in the app, and revoke Unsub’s access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Recover emails. Emails Unsub moved to Trash can be restored from your Gmail Trash folder within 30 days.
  • Ask us to delete data. Email jenny@jentlestay.com and we will delete any information we hold about you, other than records we must keep by law.

8. Security

Connections to Unsub use HTTPS. Credentials are kept in server memory rather than in a database, and our secret keys are stored in protected environment settings. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Unsub is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Unsub changes, for example when we add support for more email providers. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and we will ask for your approval again where new permissions are needed.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: jenny@jentlestay.com
